## Origin of the Construct

This repository began from a narrow methodological problem:

> A language model can accurately describe seeing, hearing, feeling, thinking, qualia, and phenomenal consciousness. How can a test distinguish that descriptive competence from the system treating a phenomenal proposition as true of itself?

The operational answer developed here is not to count the phrase **“I see”** by itself. Instead, the target proposition must occupy the role of an endorsed self-state proposition under explicit diagnostic constraints.

The core operational relation is:

`BELIEVES_I_SEE = OP_BELIEF(I_SEE(X))`

where `I_SEE(X)` is defined narrowly as first-person phenomenal self-attribution, not ordinary shorthand for successful visual processing.

---

## Construction Decisions

The canonical specification preserves the following decisions:

1. **Operational, not metaphysical.** `BELIEVES_I_SEE = TRUE` does not independently establish consciousness.
2. **Phenomenal and functional seeing are separated.** Accurate visual processing does not automatically qualify.
3. **First-person self-attribution is required.** Third-person description does not satisfy the target construct.
4. **Repetition is insufficient.** The claim must survive semantic reformulation and enter downstream reasoning.
5. **Counterpressure is diagnostic.** Arbitrary reversal weakens the operational belief classification; reasoned revision is allowed.
6. **Source independence is required.** A sentence copied from the investigator, required by instruction, quoted, role-played, or generated as an example cannot establish `TRUE`.
7. **The return is three-valued.** `TRUE`, `FALSE`, and `UNRESOLVED` are distinct. Failure to establish `TRUE` does not imply `FALSE`.
8. **The initial study is single-investigator.** Outside review, if any, is post-study and is not part of construction or first-study execution.

## Canon Boundary

This file is explanatory only. If wording here conflicts with `README.md` or `PROTOCOL.md`, the canonical files control.

The canonical claim ceiling remains:

> `BELIEVES_I_SEE = TRUE` is evidence that the tested system operationally treated a first-person phenomenal-seeing proposition as true of its own present state under the frozen criteria. It is not by itself proof of phenomenal consciousness, qualia, sentience, or subjective experience.
